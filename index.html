System Overview

Ketamine Dreams is a real-time, browser-based immersive environment built on Three.js. It generates procedurally driven dreamscapes that shift between distinct visual regimes, each with its own geometry, material behavior, and post-processing stack. The experience runs entirely client-side with peer-to-peer multi-user support, daily-seeded parameters, and a post-processing pipeline that includes bloom, edge detection, chromatic aberration, scanlines, and melt transitions.

Scene Registry

01

THE NEXUS

// nexus
Technical Description

Procedural cityscape constructed from instanced wall primitives with dynamic material assignment. Features a heart-spawning system layered atop the geometry grid, with bloom-driven emissive channels and edge-contrast post-processing. Wall materials shift hue based on the daily seed, creating a unique palette each rotation cycle.

Personal Narrative

The first corridor I ever walked felt like stepping through a membrane. Walls rose from the floor like grown things, not built. The light had weight to it. I remember thinking: this is what a dream engine looks like from the inside.

02

SPARSE SPACE

// space
Technical Description

Minimalist volumetric field with sparse geometric primitives distributed across a wide coordinate space. Relies heavily on the post-processing pipeline for visual density — chromatic aberration and scanline passes create texture where geometry is absent. Camera velocity is uncapped, encouraging high-speed traversal.

Personal Narrative

There is a quality to emptiness when it is rendered in real time. The few shapes that appear feel intentional, placed by something watching. I moved faster here than any other place. Speed became the only thing that felt real.

03

LUMBER

// lumber
Technical Description

Dense forest-like arrangement of columnar geometry with bark-textured materials. The scene uses a custom shader for organic surface variation, with fold-intensity parameters controlling the degree of geometric warping. Vein-speed uniforms drive a secondary displacement map that creates a breathing effect across vertical surfaces.

Personal Narrative

The trees here are not trees. They are columns of compressed memory, each one a record of something that almost happened. The air moves through them like a slow exhalation. I stood still for what felt like hours and the light changed color once.

04

LIMINAL

// liminal
Technical Description

Transitional space defined by low-frequency geometry and muted color channels. The scene overrides the normal bloom radius and reduces edge contrast to produce a soft, indistinct visual field. Transition melt effects are most pronounced here, as the scene is designed to blur the boundary between dream states.

Personal Narrative

This is the space between. Not a place you arrive at, but a place you pass through without noticing. The walls are close enough to touch but never quite there. I have woken up here more times than I care to count.
